The ranking you designed

Three levels, each only breaks ties

1

Feature fit

How many requested features are confirmed, unknown or contradicted. Missing evidence never counts as a mismatch, but it loses to confirmed evidence.

2

Channel agreement

A product found by the title and the attribute channel outranks one found by a single channel. RRF does this by construction.

3

Experience

Review aspects such as comfort and reliability, only where reviews exist. Fit and quality stay separate axes.

confirmed› unknown› contradicted

After that, the LLM judges product type over the final 20–30 candidates. Wrong type is removed; a missing feature sinks with its trade-off stated ("no mic, but $40 cheaper"). Every removal cites a reason, and an LLM error falls back to the deterministic order.

Review evidence rules

What a review is allowed to prove

Opinion-driven aspects

comfort · sound · fit

Only reviews that mention the aspect count. Silence means unknown. Three of three positive comfort mentions is strong; ten of forty is weak.

Complaint-driven aspects

reliability · defects · safety

People only write when it breaks, so silence counts as fine. The score is a defect rate over all product reviews, judged against the category's own baseline.

Product or noise?

Anything about the item's physical or functional state is product. Couriers, sellers, support and refunds are noise. The noise list is the same in every store, so it can be fixed once.

Dead on arrival

Crushed box and cracked screen: an unlucky unit. Perfect box and it won't turn on: counts against the product. The whole review decides the cause.

Every aspect needs at least about three mentions, and its positive share is pulled toward a neutral prior (the same Bayesian pattern the Cypher ranking already uses), so three reviews never look as certain as three hundred.

Roadmap

R1 · RRF fusion this branch

One ranked list per channel, best match per product, fused by RRF. Soft bonus rescaled. 3-vs-2-channel ablation, a tuned-sum arm, popularity-bias@K and type-precision@K.

R2 · Evaluation foundation next

Hard-case benchmark (no reviews, brand+model titles, accessory traps, trade-offs), a tuning/test split and a leakage check, before any weight is tuned.

R3 · Search order and query side planned

Filter-first or ANN-first by selectivity, rare attributes weigh more, query versions including HyDE, graded category distance.

R4 · Feature fit and the LLM judge planned

Full-text feature check, tiered ranking, product type and accessory detection, batched LLM rerank, the "good enough" gate.

R5 · Offline graph enrichment planned

Typed feature vocabulary with local-LLM labels, the review aspect pipeline, the evidence ledger.

Later · Conversation Meta-Phase B

Preference edges, two lanes and "we don't carry it", complement edges, doc2query.

Synthesis

Links across the techniques

1 → 6 → 7

"Unknown is not zero" from First Principles ran through the whole session. It became the confirmed › unknown › contradicted order, the silence rule per aspect, and the gate.

2 → 4 → 7

Explanation as a receipt, the per-product ledger and the LLM's cited removals are one idea. R1's per-channel ranks are its first column.

6 → 7

Giving reviews the verifier role made the unfinished embedding backfill irrelevant to review evidence.

1 → 8

"Product-side fan-out is the bug" is exactly what one best match per channel fixes. The query versions in R3 plug into the same fuser.

2 → 4 → 5

The accessory trap is attacked three times: product type, LLM judge and type-precision@K. The metric ships first so the other two can be measured.
