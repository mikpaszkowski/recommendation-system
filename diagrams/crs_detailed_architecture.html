1. Preference Parser & Router

Hard constraints: price, brand include / exclude, category. Soft preferences: value + polarity. Attributes the catalog cannot filter on are demoted to soft preferences.

Router actions: SEARCH · CLARIFY · UPDATE_PROFILE · READ_PROFILE · ANSWER.

Guardrail: category missing → CLARIFY. Override: category and brand given → SEARCH.

2. Multi-Index Search, Fusion & MACS

Before R1: sum(score) over every matching node, weights 1.0 / 0.8 / 0.9, 600 nodes per index. Products with many reviews or attributes win (hub bias).

R1: each product once per channel by its best match, ranked after filters, fused by RRF (k = 60). Soft bonus scaled to the fused score.

MACS: fewer than 3 results → widen the budget by 15%, then drop the category.

3. Critic & KECR Reasoning

Critic: one LLM check per candidate (20 calls). A batched listwise rerank is planned (R4, AI-08).

KECR: conversational-match and sparse-fallback paths for the top-3, with gating α (0–1).

Historical and co-purchase paths do not fire on the live graph: reviews link via ABOUT_PRODUCT without timestamps, and BOUGHT_TOGETHER does not exist (AI-03).

4. Explainable Synthesis

Prompt construction: the top-3 items and their KECR paths are injected as [GRAPH EVIDENCE]; the LLM must stay within graph facts.

R1: per-channel ranks and the fused score are recorded per candidate in eval_trace, the first column of the planned evidence ledger.

Roadmap: R2 benchmark · R3 search order & HyDE · R4 feature fit & LLM judge · R5 labels & review aspects.
